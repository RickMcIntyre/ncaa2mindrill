Two-Minute Drill

College football · FBS 2023–2025

Loading three seasons of FBS drives, most of which did not go well…

Every snap is drawn from a real 2023–2025 FBS play. Head coaches as of the game played.

One drive a day

  1. Take over a real drive. Each day is a real late-game FBS drive from 2023–2025: the real score, clock, field position, timeouts and players.
  2. Call every snap. Runs, passes by depth and side, spikes, field goals. Each result is a real college snap from the same kind of situation, nudged by how good both teams were that season.
  3. Manage the clock. College rules: the clock stops on incompletions, out of bounds, and briefly on first downs inside the final two minutes. Burn a timeout when the clock is running.
  4. Beat the real coach. When your drive ends, your win chance is compared with what the real head coach got.
  5. Saban mode. A second daily drive from the hopeless end of the pool: 10% to win or worse, or two scores down. Nobody is expected to win these.
  6. Cignetti mode. One shelf up: 10–25% to win. Hard, not hopeless. Google yourself afterward.
