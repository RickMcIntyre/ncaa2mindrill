## 2. r/CFB post

**Title:** I built a daily game where you take over a real late-game college drive and get graded against the coach who actually called it

**Body:**

It's at ncaa2mindrill.com and it's free. Every day is one real FBS drive from 2023–2025: real score, clock, field position, timeouts, and the players who were on the field. You call every snap (full route tree for every receiver, runs with direction or a tapped hole, play action, screens, spikes, timeouts, tempo). Each result is drawn from a real college snap of the same type and situation, tilted by how good both teams were that season; the defense shows a look before each play; penalties, scrambles and strip sacks happen at the measured rates. Every snap animates on the field.

At the end you're compared with the real head coach: your win chance after your drive vs. his after his, snap by snap. You also get a percentile against 300 simulated play-callers and the real coach whose play-calling tendencies you resemble.

Honest caveats: it's a model. Players are rated by their season EPA, not matchups. The win-probability model is fitted on three seasons of Q4 FBS plays and is calibrated, but it's still a model. The animation is a drawing of what happened, not tracking data. It's fan-made and not affiliated with anyone.

There are two paid daily drives ($0.99, once) from the ugly end of the pool: Cignetti mode (10–25% to win) and Saban mode (10% or worse, or two scores down). The rest stays free.

I'd love to hear which drives you want in the pool, and where the model is wrong.
