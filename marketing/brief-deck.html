Every result makes this 1200×630 card, sized for X, iMessage and group chats.

The verdict

Your win chance after your drive vs. the coach's after his. "I beat Steve Sarkisian 88%–4%" is the hook.

Snap by snap

Your calls next to the coach's actual calls, each graded 🟩🟨🟥 against the best option on the board.

Percentile

300 simulated college play-callers replay the same drive. You get a rank and a dry line to go with it.

"You call it like…"

Your pass rate overall, on early downs and on passing downs, matched to a real FBS head coach's tendencies.

03 · It moves

Every snap is animated, with popups

  • Receivers run the routes you called; corners mirror them; safeties play their shell; linebackers drop then pursue.
  • The ball flies with a real arc and lands where the pass was actually caught; the catch becomes run-after-catch.
  • Backs hit the hole you tapped; the defense converges on the real spot of the tackle. Tosses pitch, draws fake, counters cut back.
  • Popups on the moment: Intercepted!Pick six!Strip sack!Mossed him!Juked him!Tackle for loss!House call!Flag
  • Three to five seconds a play. Tap the field to skip.

The run game: tap where you want him to hit it. The crosshair is the hole; the concept is how he gets there.

04 · Voice

Dry. Literal. Deadpan.

"The offensive line would like to be excluded from this narrative."
"Incomplete. The clock stops for this, per rule 3-3-2, roughly."
"He ran that one straight into a meeting."
"Three points. He will be left alone now, as kickers prefer."
"You out-called James Franklin. He has a staff. You have a browser."

Do

  • Short sentences. One idea per post.
  • Specific over clever. Real numbers only.
  • Jokes land on the user, the moment, or football itself.

Don't

  • Dunk on a named coach or player. We compare; we don't pile on.
  • Hype, exclamation points, "trending", fake quotes or stats.
  • Emoji in body copy (grade squares in share text are the one exception).
